## Lessons Learned
I learned a lot of things during this lab, as it was my first time ever experiencing and using a 3d printer. One simple thing I learned was how to use a flash drive. I have never had one, let alone needed to use one, so I didn't know how to download something onto the flash drive to transfer it to the machine for printing. I also learned that you could put multiple projects and downloads onto one PrusaSlicer plater. This is my first time using PrusaSlicer, so I was shown that we could put all three of me and my groupmates' and project on one plater. Something I found interesting that I learned was that you can simply scale down a download, and it doesn't affect how it's printed. I was making an assumption from my knowledge of CAD that when you shrink something, it could mess up you feature and cause extrudes and cuts to intertwine and not come out right. Normally, you have to dimension before you can cut stuff down, so just being able to scale the entire download down was surprising to me. Lastly, I learned about the different filaments and their uses when printing with them. In this class, we use PETG and PLA. PLA is a biodegradable plant based fillament the prints at a lower temperature than PETG, but it comes out more rigid and has lower heat resistance. PETG, however, is more flexible and better suited for printing things for outdoor use with its higher heat resistance. PETG is mechanically stronger than PLA, having higher tensile strength, but it is also slightly more expensive. Both, however, are relatively cheap.
