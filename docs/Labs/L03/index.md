# A3 – Design Something Small

## Design
For my self-design, I wanted to make a small cracker. Similar to a Ritz cracker but smaller to fit the maximum dimension criteria we were given. I chose this because we were given basic instructions to design something small with no stipulations except to be school-appropriate.
#### Criteria
-No more than 0.5in tall
-No overhangs
-Large enough to use an infil
-Modify the wall thickness
-Print in PLA or PETG
-No more than 1.5in by 1.5in
-Print time must be less than 1.5 hours
